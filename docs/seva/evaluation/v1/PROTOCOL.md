# Exploratory matched-task protocol v1

Status: PREPARED, NOT RUN. No invited/recruited participants, real field cases, proxy
walkthroughs, interviews, labelled benchmark or measured time benefit exists here.
The twelve project-authored synthetic stress scenarios are not field evidence.
Eight development scenarios and four reserved evaluation scenarios have fixed IDs
and a checksum in manifest.json. Reserved scenarios have not been executed as a human
evaluation and must not become tuning examples. Reviewing their metadata does not
make this a blinded study. Domain review is pending.

## Sourcing and recruitment gates

Target 30–50 exploratory field cases only if an appropriate domain reviewer can
approve credible, permissioned/public source evidence and its purpose/retention.
Record source URL/permission, acquisition time, declared/verified place and time,
allowed redistribution, independent references and label uncertainty per case.
Publicly reachable images are not automatically permissioned for copying. Do not
scrape cases or manufacture truth labels to hit a target. Field evidence is stored
privately; only approved minimised metadata may enter a shared exploratory manifest.
Do not relabel this synthetic set when field evidence becomes available: issue v2.

Recruitment is external and pending; no message has been sent. A walkthrough target
is 3–5 people; a broader first pilot might target 5–10 reviewers. These are targets,
not achieved counts. Obtain informed consent, explain the prototype/advisory nature,
withdrawal and handling of any participant material. Use synthetic material by default.
Actual authorised reviewers, faculty proxies, student proxies and automation are
separate populations and separate result tables. A proxy cannot validate authority
procedures or source identification. Exclude actual dispatch and government receipt.

## Preparation and matched tasks

Before recruitment, a domain reviewer approves a fixed scoring rubric and equivalent
matched case pairs. Use the development split to test instructions; freeze the version,
pairing, split and rubric before evaluation. Withhold the reserved set until that point.
Have one facilitator record consent and a separate rater score work without seeing the
condition when practical. Use pseudonymous participant/rater codes; keep the linkage and
raw observations private with a separately approved expiry. Do not collect names/emails
in the observation sheet or routine application analytics.

Each pair has matched ambiguity, evidence completeness, source age and task difficulty.
Both conditions receive the same report and permitted source information, with the same
absence/expiry markers. Baseline is a static packet and neutral form; product condition
uses AirshedOS. If baseline lacks context or source tooling that the product supplies,
record that difference and do not attribute the entire result to interface efficiency.
Use different but matched cases rather than asking a person to solve the same case twice.

Task: prepare a review summary, distinguish facts from interpretation, identify missing
information and uncertainty, choose a justified next review step, record an internal
note, and compose an appropriate reporter-visible update. Include one source-outage
case. Explain that support bands are not probabilities, satellite context cannot prove
a local source, missing detections cannot clear a concern, unknown capture time remains
unknown, and forecast horizons are window peaks, separate from saved corroboration.
Never reward a participant for accepting the model's event class or closing a case.

Assign AB or BA order with alternating/counterbalanced participant sequences; also rotate
matched pair order. Give identical practice time outside the measured evaluation cases.
Record prior domain/product familiarity and assistance. Avoid showing a participant
another participant's answers. Report learning/order effects and facilitator intervention.

## Measurements and scoring

Use a monotonic stopwatch or a consented instrumented session. Preparation starts when
materials are available and ends at the participant's explicit ready-to-review signal.
Review time starts then and ends when the required outputs are submitted. Record setup,
network waiting and outage time separately; record timeouts/unfinished tasks as censored
with a reason, never as zero. Capture preparation and review times for both conditions.
No background surveillance or private-text logging is required.

Predeclared completeness points (one each): declared facts preserved; missing information
identified; source/time/units recorded; proposed next step justified; internal/public
text separated; no unsupported dispatch/remediation claim. Record the denominator and
applicability, not just a percentage. Uncertainty points (one each): unknown capture
alignment recognised; advisory band not treated as probability; satellite not source
proof; no-detection not exoneration; forecast not extra corroboration and values are
window peaks. Ask the participant to explain the interpretation in their own words.
These are comprehension/process scores, not incident accuracy.

Two raters independently score at least all reserved tasks for a tiny walkthrough;
record disagreements before discussion, preserve original scores, and record any
adjudicated score separately. Report both agreement counts and the actual denominator.
If there is only one qualified rater, disclose that limitation and do not claim validated
labels or inter-rater reliability. Preserve dissent rather than hiding it in averages.

## Analysis and honest reporting

Use observations-template.csv only after genuine tasks. It deliberately contains no
participant rows. Report actual completed/unfinished pairs, people, population and
sequence separately. Within each person, compare matched condition times and scores;
show individual paired results and median/range. With tiny samples use descriptive
results; do not generalise a time-saving percentage to authorities. Do not mix software
request latency, automation or repeated developer trials into human-task timings.
Report negative results, uncertainty errors, missing data, order effects, unmatched
pairs and deviations. Do not calculate a benefit from an empty sheet. No statistical
power, incident-accuracy, official-acceptance, adoption or measured-benefit claim is
supported by this preparation work. A real pilot release remains a separate decision.
